"How long is it?" is one of the first questions for any big RPG, and Rebel Wolves has been refreshingly clear about The Blood of Dawnwalker. Here's exactly what the developers have said about playtime — and what actually drives those numbers.

## The short answer

- **Main story:** roughly **30–40 hours**, per the creative director.
- **Average full playthrough:** around **50 hours**.
- **Explorers / completionists:** **60–70 hours** or more.

So most players should expect a **substantial but not bloated** RPG — long enough to feel epic, short enough to actually finish.

## Quality over size

Importantly, Rebel Wolves has said the world **"isn't that big."** That's by design. A more contained map let the team **"go crazy with actual content,"** filling Vale Sangora with hand-crafted quests and reactivity instead of empty space and busywork. In other words, the hours you spend are meant to be **dense**, not padded.

This fits the game's whole philosophy as a [narrative sandbox](/guides/gameplay-systems-explained/) where your choices and the time of day shape how quests resolve.

## What changes your playtime

Your personal total comes down to a few things:

- **Exploration** — Vale Sangora rewards poking around; thorough players add many hours.
- **Side quests** — the [relationship](/guides/romance-guide/) and faction threads can pull you off the main path.
- **The time system** — because [time is a resource](/guides/30-day-time-limit-explained/), how you spend days affects how much you see in one run.
- **Difficulty and gear** — tougher settings and more upgrading stretch combat encounters.

## Will there be replay value?

Given the branching choices, the [infamy system](/guides/infamy-system-explained/), and time-sensitive quests that can resolve differently by day or night, a single run won't show you everything. Expect meaningful reasons to start a second playthrough with different decisions.
