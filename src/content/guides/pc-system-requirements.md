Rebel Wolves has published the official PC system requirements for The Blood of Dawnwalker, and the good news is they're more reasonable than a brand-new, visually ambitious 2026 RPG might have been. Here's the full breakdown, plus what the numbers actually mean for your rig.

## Minimum — 1080p, 30 FPS, Low settings

| Component | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| CPU | Intel Core i7-8700K / AMD Ryzen 7 3700X |
| GPU | RTX 3050 / GTX 1070 / RX Vega 56 / Intel Arc A580 (6GB VRAM) |
| RAM | 16 GB |
| OS | Windows 10, DirectX 12 |
| Storage | 60 GB SSD |

## Recommended — 1080p, 60 FPS, High settings

| Component | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| CPU | Intel Core i5-13600 / AMD Ryzen 9 7900X |
| GPU | RTX 5060 / RX 6800 XT (12GB VRAM) |
| RAM | 16 GB |
| OS | Windows 10, DirectX 12 |
| Storage | 60 GB SSD |

## What these numbers mean for you

- **16GB RAM across the board.** If you're still on 16GB you're fine, but with a modern OS and background apps, 32GB is the comfortable choice for headroom.
- **An SSD is mandatory.** The 60GB requirement specifies SSD — an old hard drive isn't supported, and a fast NVMe drive will help with streaming in a quest-dense world.
- **VRAM is the real gatekeeper.** 6GB scrapes the minimum; 12GB is the recommended target. VRAM, more than raw GPU horsepower, is what tends to cause stutter in modern open-region RPGs.
- **DLSS and FSR change the math.** The official targets are *native* rendering with no frame generation. Turn on DLSS or FSR upscaling and you'll comfortably beat the listed frame rates — a mid-range card can realistically aim higher than the chart suggests.

## Bottom line

If your PC is from roughly the last five years with a 6GB+ GPU and an SSD, you'll be able to run it. For a smooth 60fps High experience, you're looking at a recent mid-range build.
