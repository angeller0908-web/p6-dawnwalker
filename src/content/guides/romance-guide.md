Romance in The Blood of Dawnwalker is shaping up to be more than a dialogue-tree checkbox. Rebel Wolves is tracking relationships at a granular level and tying them into the game's time system, which makes courting someone a genuine investment. Here's everything confirmed so far — the developers are keeping specifics under wraps, so this is the framework, not a list of who to date.

## Relationships are tracked on a micro level

Beyond the broad [infamy/reputation system](/guides/infamy-system-explained/), Rebel Wolves maintains a **database of Coen's individual relationships**. Every meaningful interaction nudges where you stand with a person, and some of those threads can develop into **romance**.

## You build bonds through action, not just talk

This is the part that sets it apart. You don't only raise affection through dialogue choices — you build relationships through **what you do**:

- **Flashy feats** — helping someone with combat or magic, showing strength.
- **Quiet acts** — calm, subtle interactions where you simply help a person with their problem.

Different characters respond to different approaches, so the way you carry yourself — bold vampire or measured human — colors who warms to you.

## Time is the real currency

Because the game runs on a **calendar/time structure** (tied to the [30-day framing](/guides/30-day-time-limit-explained/) and the day/night cycle), spending time with someone is a real choice. An evening invested in a relationship is an evening not spent on a quest. That scarcity is meant to make bonds feel earned rather than farmed.

## What the developers won't say yet

Rebel Wolves has been **deliberately quiet about specific romance options**, stating only that they exist and that they're crafting rich characters worth pursuing. That's arguably a good sign — it suggests romances are woven into the story rather than bolted on.

## Our advice for now

You can't plan a romance route before launch, but you can prepare your mindset:

- **Pay attention to characters early** — relationships build over time, so the sooner you invest, the deeper they can go.
- **Match your approach to the person** — some want a protector, some want a confidant.
- **Budget your nights** — time is finite, so decide which bonds matter to you.

We'll publish a full, character-by-character romance walkthrough once the game launches and the options are known. Until then, track new reveals in our [News feed](/news/).
