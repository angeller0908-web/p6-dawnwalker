When the sun goes down, Coen stops being a swordsman and becomes a predator. His nighttime kit is fast, mobile, and stealth-focused — a completely different game from his [daytime swordplay](/guides/combat-and-hex-magic/). Here's every confirmed vampire ability and how to think about using it.

## Core vampire abilities

- **Shadowstep** — a short-range teleport. Close distance on a target, escape danger, or reposition instantly.
- **Wall-walking** — Coen can climb **vertically up walls**, opening approach routes that human Coen simply can't reach.
- **Claws** — your nighttime weapon: fast, aggressive melee instead of the measured sword.
- **Bite / feeding** — biting enemies **regenerates your health**. Your sustain comes from feeding, so the predator stays in the fight by hunting.

## The wolf transformation

The standout night power is the **wolf form.** At night Coen can **transform into a wolf to move much faster**, covering ground and slipping through the world more freely than on two legs.

The wolf isn't just for travel. You can **transition straight from wolf form into human form to perform "Death from Above"** — dropping onto an unsuspecting enemy to **assassinate them before a fight even begins.** It turns the wolf into a stealth-approach tool: run in fast and low as the wolf, then strike from above.

## Stealth is the night's golden rule

Power comes with a cost: as a vampire you're **hunted.** Both guards *and* civilians can spot you, and a high profile feeds your [infamy](/guides/infamy-system-explained/), bringing curfews and bounty hunters. So nighttime play rewards:

- Using **Shadowstep and wall-walking** to approach from unexpected angles.
- Using the **wolf + Death from Above** to remove threats silently.
- **Feeding** to recover without prolonged, noisy brawls.

## How to play the night

Think of the night as a stealth-action sandbox: scout with the wolf, pick off isolated targets with Death from Above, and only commit to open claw combat when you have to. Pair this with your [daytime build](/guides/combat-and-hex-magic/) and three [skill trees](/guides/builds-and-skill-trees/) to decide whether you lean predator, swordsman, or hybrid.
