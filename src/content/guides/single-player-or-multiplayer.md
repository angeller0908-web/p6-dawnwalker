If you're wondering whether you can play The Blood of Dawnwalker with friends, here's the clear answer.

## The short answer: single-player only

**The Blood of Dawnwalker is a fully single-player game.** There is **no multiplayer, no co-op, and no PvP.** The Steam page and every official description list it as a single-player action RPG.

## Why it's built that way

This isn't a limitation — it's the point. The entire design leans on things that only work in a focused solo experience:

- **A personal story.** You are Coen, racing to save your family. That intimacy doesn't translate to a party of players.
- **Deep choice and consequence.** The [infamy system](/guides/infamy-system-explained/) and branching quests track *your* decisions; shared decision-making would dilute them.
- **The day/night identity.** Being [human by day and vampire by night](/guides/gameplay-systems-explained/) is a single-character fantasy, with a [time system](/guides/30-day-time-limit-explained/) you manage alone.
- **Relationships and romance.** The [relationship system](/guides/romance-guide/) is built around Coen's personal bonds.

## What this means for you

Expect a **handcrafted, narrative-first RPG** in the tradition of the studio's Witcher 3 roots — see how the two [compare here](/guides/vs-the-witcher-3/). You'll spend your time fully inhabiting one character and one story, with no online requirements to enjoy the campaign.
