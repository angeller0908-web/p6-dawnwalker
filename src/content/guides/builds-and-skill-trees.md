Because Coen is two characters in one body, building him is about deciding **which half you want to be stronger** — and when. The Blood of Dawnwalker gives you **three skill trees** and skill points to spend on active abilities and passive bonuses. Here's how to approach it before launch.

## How the skill system works

As you progress you **earn skill points**, which you invest across **three distinct skill trees** to unlock:

- **Active abilities** — new tools you use in combat and traversal.
- **Passive bonuses** — stat boosts and quality-of-life perks that quietly strengthen your playstyle.

The exact node names aren't public yet, but the structure points clearly toward three archetypes.

## The three build archetypes

### ☀️ The Swordsman (day-focused)
Lean into [daytime combat](/guides/combat-and-hex-magic/): directional dueling, **Hex magic**, and **executions**. Best if you enjoy methodical, defensive fights and want raw human power. You'll talk and fight your way through problems in daylight.

### 🌙 The Predator (night-focused)
Maximize your [vampire powers](/guides/vampire-powers-and-wolf-form/): **Shadowstep**, wall-walking, feeding, and the **wolf form** with Death from Above. Best if you love stealth, mobility, and picking enemies off before they know you're there.

### ⚖️ The Hybrid (flexible)
Spread investment so you're capable in both states. You give up some peak power in exchange for **always having the right tool** for whatever the clock — and the quest — demands. Given how much the [time system](/guides/30-day-time-limit-explained/) drives the game, flexibility is genuinely valuable.

## How to plan your points

- **Pick a fantasy early.** Spreading points thin across all three trees leaves you weak everywhere.
- **Match your build to how you like to solve problems** — social and direct (day) vs stealthy and predatory (night).
- **Account for the clock.** If you plan to push the main story fast, a focused build shines; if you'll explore widely across day and night, hybrid pays off.
