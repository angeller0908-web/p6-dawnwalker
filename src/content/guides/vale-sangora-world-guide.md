The Blood of Dawnwalker unfolds in one carefully built place: **Vale Sangora.** Understanding the world — and how the game wants you to move through it — sets you up to get the most out of every play session. Here's a spoiler-free guide to the setting and exploration.

## Where and when

Vale Sangora is a fictional valley nestled **somewhere in the Carpathian Mountains** of Central/Southeastern Europe, in the year **1347**. It's a grim, folkloric dark fantasy: **plague** ravages the population while the ancient vampire lord **Brencis** rules from above, levying a brutal **blood tax** on the living. For the full story setup, see our [Story, Setting & Characters guide](/guides/story-setting-characters/).

## A "narrative sandbox," not a giant map

Rebel Wolves deliberately avoids "open world." They've said the world **"isn't that big"** — and that's a feature. A contained valley let the team **"go crazy with actual content,"** packing the space with hand-crafted quests, characters, and reactivity rather than filler. The payoff: exploration that consistently *means something*, and a tighter, denser RPG (see our [length guide](/guides/how-long-to-beat/)).

## Day and night change how you explore

The single most important thing about traversing Vale Sangora is that **time of day transforms it:**

- **By day (human Coen):** you move openly, talk to NPCs, gather information, and find peaceful routes through situations.
- **By night (vampire Coen):** you gain [mobility and power](/guides/vampire-powers-and-wolf-form/) — wall-walking, Shadowstep, and the wolf form for fast travel — but you're hunted, so stealth matters and your [infamy](/guides/infamy-system-explained/) can rise.

The same location can offer completely different opportunities depending on when you visit it.

## How to explore well

- **Revisit places at different times.** A path closed to human Coen may open to the wolf at night, and vice versa.
- **Mind the clock.** Because [time is a resource](/guides/30-day-time-limit-explained/), explore with intent rather than aimlessly.
- **Follow the people.** In a dense, reactive world, NPCs and side stories are where the richest content hides.
