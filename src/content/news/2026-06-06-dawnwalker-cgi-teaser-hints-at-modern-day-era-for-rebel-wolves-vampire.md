Rebel Wolves surprised dark fantasy fans at Summer Game Fest 2026 with a dual-trailer reveal that significantly expands the scope of their upcoming action-RPG. While the new gameplay trailer showcases the visceral combat and plague-ravaged 14th-century setting of the debut chapter, a secondary CGI teaser introduces a massive curveball: the franchise plans to span multiple centuries, continents, and cultures, even reportedly showing protagonist Coen in the modern day.

## A Century-Spanning Saga in the Making

According to an update from developer Rebel Wolves, the team aims to establish a much larger narrative universe than previously assumed. Game Director Konrad Tomaszkiewicz clarified that while *The Blood of Dawnwalker* itself tells a self-contained story, it serves as the opening chapter of a saga that will eventually move far beyond its initial medieval European setting. 

The most surprising element of this reveal came during a second trailer, which reportedly depicts the vampire protagonist, Coen, slouched in a chair in the present day. This temporal jump indicates that the franchise's upcoming installments are expected to follow Coen or other vrakhiri through different historical eras and global locations. Rather than remaining a traditional medieval fantasy, the series is positioning itself as a historical and urban fantasy epic.

## What the Gameplay Trailer Reveals About the First Chapter

The gameplay trailer shown during the IGN Summer of Gaming event provides a closer look at the immediate threat facing players when the game launches. Set in 14th-century Europe, the story unfolds against the backdrop of the Black Plague. Players will guide the vrakhiri as they rise up against human rule to claim their own freedom and power. 

Key details highlighted in the footage and official materials include:
- **Atmospheric World Design:** The trailer showcases a copper-toned, bleak landscape that captures the grim tone of plague-era Europe.
- **Vampiric Combat:** Players can expect a mix of traditional weaponry and supernatural abilities, building upon what we know of the game's [combat and hex magic](/guides/combat-and-hex-magic/).
- **Self-Contained Narrative:** Despite the teasing of a modern-day future, the story of *The Blood of Dawnwalker* is confirmed to be fully resolved within this first release.

## Release Date and Collector's Edition Details

Rebel Wolves and publisher Bandai Namco re-confirmed that the game is scheduled for release on September 3, 2026. The title will launch simultaneously on PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S. 

For dedicated collectors, the developers also shared a first look at the physical Collector’s Edition via an unboxing video. Pre-orders for all editions are now live on the official website. Those looking for more details on pricing and platform availability can check out our breakdown of the [release date, platforms, and editions](/guides/release-date-platforms-editions/).

Given the studio's pedigree—founded by former CD Projekt Red developers—comparisons to [The Witcher 3](/guides/vs-the-witcher-3/) are inevitable. However, this new teaser suggests Rebel Wolves is aiming for an entirely different structural format, trading a single fantasy realm for a century-spanning chronicle of vampire history.
