Recent community discussions have shed light on the prominent figures controlling the dark corners of Vale Sangora. According to a set of details shared on community forums, Rebel Wolves' upcoming dark fantasy RPG *The Blood of Dawnwalker* features four distinct Vampire Lords. 

These figures reportedly form the backbone of the game's aristocratic hierarchy, bringing their own historical baggages and conflicting ambitions to the narrative sandbox.

## Brencis: The Roman Senator Turned Tyrant

At the apex of this vampiric hierarchy stands Brencis, who serves as the central antagonist of the story. According to the community-reported lore, Brencis is an ancient vampire born in AD 131 during the Roman Empire, where he once served as a senator. After his transformation, he suffered a severe fall from grace, losing his societal standing. 

In the modern timeline of the game, he has clawed his way back to power, ruling [Vale Sangora](/guides/vale-sangora-world-guide) with extreme cruelty. His regime is defined by a brutal "blood tax" imposed on the local human population. Players familiar with his shadow will likely recognize his heavy influence over the region's political climate, as detailed in our analysis of [Brencis and the Vampire Officers](/guides/brencis-and-vampire-officers).

## Bakir: The Khan of the Shadows

Perhaps the most striking new figure is Bakir, described as a ruthless and highly ambitious lord with a Mongol-inspired backstory. Often likened by community members to a vampiric Genghis Khan, Bakir represents a militaristic faction within the undead aristocracy. 

Lore snippets suggest that Bakir is not content with playing second fiddle to Brencis. Rumors point toward a major betrayal arc where Bakir attempts to subvert Brencis's rule, presenting players with a volatile political landscape where alliances might shift rapidly.

## Xanthe: The Aristocratic Powerhouse

Representing the traditional nobility of the undead is Xanthe. Widely regarded as one of the most powerful vampire lords in the region, Xanthe holds a prominent position within Vale Sangora's vampiric high society. Unlike the brutal warlord styling of Bakir or the tyrannical desperation of Brencis, Xanthe appears to wield power through established aristocratic structures and pure influence.

## Ambress: The Rebellious Pawn

The youngest and weakest of the four lords is Ambress (closely associated with the naming of [Ambrus](/guides/2026-06-23-who-is-ambrus-the-blood-of-dawnwalker-s-most-ambitious-vampire-officer)). Having been turned only recently, he finds himself struggling to adapt to his new reality. 

Ambress is reportedly locked in conflict with Coen and is actively resisting being used as a disposable pawn in Brencis’s larger schemes. This makes him a highly unpredictable variable in the region's power dynamics—and potentially a key point of leverage for players looking to dismantle the vampire hegemony from within.
