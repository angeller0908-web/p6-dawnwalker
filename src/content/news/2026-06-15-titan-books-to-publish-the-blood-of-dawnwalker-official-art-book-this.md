Rebel Wolves is partnering with Titan Books to release *The Blood of Dawnwalker: The Art of the Game*. Scheduled for release in November 2026, this official art book will document the visual design of the upcoming dark fantasy RPG, which launches on September 3, 2026.

According to a community listing, the hardcover book will feature concept art, behind-the-scenes material, and exclusive developer interviews outlining how the team built its grim atmosphere. 

## Documenting the Lore of Vale Sangora

For players looking forward to exploring the gothic landscapes in the [vale-sangora-world-guide](/guides/vale-sangora-world-guide/), the art book promises to show the evolution of the game’s environment design. Early concept sketches will highlight how the developers transitioned from initial pitch ideas to the final dark fantasy aesthetic.

The volume is also expected to include design sheets for the game's unique creatures and factions. This includes the [Vrakhiri](/guides/2026-06-11-who-are-the-vrakhiri-how-rebel-wolves-reinvents-vampire-lore-in-dawnwa/), the monstrous vampire race that serves as a core part of the game's lore. The illustrations will likely showcase the evolution of the game's dark magic systems, which the studio recently described as having a low-fantasy, paranormal tone in their [magic preview](/guides/2026-06-12-rebel-wolves-teases-paranormal-low-fantasy-magic-for-the-blood-of-dawn/).

## Release and Pre-Order Information

While Rebel Wolves has already detailed some physical editions for the game itself in their [physical launch details](/guides/2026-06-09-the-blood-of-dawnwalker-physical-launch-details-day-one-steelbooks-ama/), this art book is a separate release handled directly by Titan Books. It is slated to arrive a few months after the game’s September launch, making it a late-autumn addition for collectors. Pre-orders are expected to open at major book retailers in the coming weeks.
