Rebel Wolves is gearing up for the home stretch. With the [confirmed launch date of September 3, 2026](/guides/launch-date-september-3-confirmed/) drawing closer, the studio is reportedly bringing in content creators for hands-on sessions. 

According to reports from the community, multiple YouTubers and influencers have been flown out to participate in a dedicated playtest event for *The Blood of Dawnwalker*. While details of what they actually played remain under lock and key, the move suggests that public hands-on impressions and raw gameplay footage might be just around the corner.

## The Preview Cycle is Nearing

For a project that has mostly relied on CGI teasers and controlled media drops, opening the doors to content creators is a significant step. So far, our best look at the game's actual mechanics came from the [Game Informer combat preview](/guides/2026-06-04-the-blood-of-dawnwalker-combat-game-informer-preview-reveals-immersive/), which shed light on how Rebel Wolves plans to combine traditional swordplay with supernatural abilities. 

With creators now getting physical hands-on time, we can expect a wave of unfiltered impressions detailing the actual feel of the [combat and hex magic systems](/guides/combat-and-hex-magic/) in real-time. Whether these playtests cover the early hours of the campaign or drop players directly into the gothic wilderness of Vale Sangora remains to be seen. 

## What This Means for the Launch Roadmap

Hosting creator playtests in late June fits perfectly with a standard summer marketing campaign. If the feedback is positive, Rebel Wolves will likely use these impressions to build momentum heading into July and August, potentially leading to a public demo or a major showcase at events like Gamescom.

Historically, letting external players touch the build indicates a level of confidence in the game's stability and performance. Given the high stakes for Rebel Wolves' debut RPG, these upcoming previews will be the first true test of whether the studio can deliver on the massive pedigree of its veteran development team.

Stay tuned as we track when the embargo lifts and what these first hands-on impressions reveal about the dark fantasy world.
