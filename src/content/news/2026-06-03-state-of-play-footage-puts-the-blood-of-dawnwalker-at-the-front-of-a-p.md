## Leading the Charge in a Crowded Month

Sony's latest State of Play broadcast gave RPG enthusiasts their first proper look at actual footage for Rebel Wolves' upcoming dark fantasy title. Along with the gameplay reveal, the developer confirmed that the game will hit shelves on September 3, 2026. This announcement positions the debut project from the ex-Witcher developers at the very start of what is shaping up to be an incredibly dense autumn release window. For more details on what is included in the initial launch, see our guide on the [release date and editions](/guides/release-date-platforms-editions/).

With major publishers reportedly shifting schedules to avoid clashing with upcoming blockbusters like Grand Theft Auto VI, September 2026 has become a bottleneck. *The Blood of Dawnwalker* is set to launch first, followed shortly by high-profile games including *Marvel’s Wolverine*, *Silent Hill: Townfall*, and *Control: Resonant*. This crowded calendar means the game's initial reception will be critical to sustaining player interest before competing titles dominate media coverage.

## What the State of Play Footage Revealed

The new trailer offered a clearer view of the combat dynamics and environmental design. While earlier updates laid out the basic [gameplay systems explained](/guides/gameplay-systems-explained/) by the developers, this video provided raw footage of combat pacing and spellcasting in action. 

Players can expect a mix of melee strikes and hex-based magic. The footage highlighted the transition between human daytime exploration and nighttime vampire encounters, which remains one of the core mechanics driving character progression. How these combat elements interact with the game's broader [builds and skill trees](/guides/builds-and-skill-trees/) will likely determine whether the combat loop holds up over the course of the story. For a deeper breakdown of the spells shown, read our analysis on [combat and hex magic](/guides/combat-and-hex-magic/).

## PC Storefront Availability and GOG Concerns

Following the State of Play showcase, PC players have raised questions about where they can purchase the game. While the title is expected to launch on major PC storefronts, a lack of active pre-order listings on GOG has caused some concern among DRM-free advocates in the community. 

Some players are speculating that the game might initially favor Steam or Epic Games Store, with a GOG release arriving later, though Bandai Namco has not yet clarified their launch-day distribution strategy for PC. As we get closer to the September release, official confirmation on platform-specific storefronts should settle whether DRM-free players can get their hands on the game on day one.
