Mastering the directional swordplay in *The Blood of Dawnwalker* requires more than just precise reflexes; it demands the right steel. While Coen’s vampiric powers and expanding hex magic provide a formidable magical arsenal, your primary melee weapon remains the cornerstone of any effective combat setup. As players continue mapping out the vast, dark fantasy expanse of Vale Sangora, a community-reported list of Legendary Swords has emerged, detailing the exact locations of some of the most powerful armaments available in the game.

Finding these top-tier weapons is rarely a simple matter of walking to a marker on your map. Rebel Wolves intricately tied much of the best gear to their narrative sandbox. Securing a legendary blade means you must see specific side quests through to the end, manipulate the complex Infamy system, or invest heavily in particular Witchcraft perks. Whether you lean into a brute-force physical build or need a swift blade to complement your nighttime skirmishes, tracking down these unique weapons is highly recommended.

Based on extensive community testing and map exploration, here are the exact locations and unlock requirements for the Legendary Swords discovered so far.

## Weapons Tied to Bounties and the Infamy System

The environment of Vale Sangora reacts aggressively to your moral choices. The local authorities do not tolerate mass chaos, and nothing demonstrates this design philosophy better than the Infamy system, which directly controls access to one of the most brutal swords in the game.

### The Purge
- **Location:** Briar Sloughs (Northern Sector)
- **Requirement:** Reach Infamy Level 7

If you want to wield **The Purge**, you have to be willing to make significant enemies within the established order. This sword is reportedly locked behind a dynamic world event triggered purely by your criminal reputation. Once you hit Infamy Level 7, a royal decree is officially issued by Brencis, the high-ranking vampire officer tasked with enforcing martial law. This decree spawns elite headhunters who will actively track Coen across the regional map. 

To claim the blade, you must locate and defeat Yaromil the Houndmaster, an elite bounty hunter expected to spawn in the northern reaches of the Briar Sloughs. The swamps are already treacherous with toxic bogs and monstrous wildlife, making a boss fight against an elite tracker a genuine test of your stamina management and your grasp of [Combat and Hex Magic](/guides/combat-and-hex-magic/). Defeating Yaromil allows you to loot The Purge directly from his body. It stands as a perfect trophy for a high-chaos playthrough. Be sure to weigh the consequences of maintaining maximum heat by reviewing our [Infamy System Explained](/guides/infamy-system-explained/) guide before committing.

## Quest-Locked Legendary Swords

A significant portion of the game’s finest gear is woven naturally into side narratives. These are not basic fetch tasks; they require deep dives into local lore, navigating hidden dungeons, and managing sensitive interactions with key NPCs.

### Durandal
- **Location:** Briar Sloughs
- **Requirement:** Complete "The Cursed Chasm" side quest.

Expected to be one of the earlier Legendary weapons you can acquire if you brave the southeastern swamps, **Durandal** serves as the ultimate reward for clearing out The Cursed Chasm. The quest mandates navigating a hazardous underground cave network. This environment specifically limits your movement, forcing you to manage Coen's daylight human limitations carefully before rewarding you with this heavy, high-damage sword.

### The Hand of Fate
- **Location:** Svartrau
- **Requirement:** Complete steps in "A Hero’s End".

Securing this sword requires a dedicated tomb-raiding effort. During the side quest "A Hero’s End," players must breach the heavily guarded treasury near the Svartrau Cathedral to acquire a specific Stone Key. Once the key is in your inventory, you must travel to the Ancient Ruins situated just outside the city limits. Using the Stone Key opens a hidden sarcophagus chamber where **The Hand of Fate** rests. Exploring Svartrau’s oppressive religious sites safely is detailed further in our [Vale Sangora World Guide](/guides/vale-sangora-world-guide/).

### Erkas’s Sword
- **Location:** Maragir Wealds
- **Requirement:** Complete "The Champion of Old" questline.

The dense forests of the Maragir Wealds hide many secrets, and completing the lengthy "The Champion of Old" questline awards you with **Erkas’s Sword**. This multi-stage quest reportedly involves piecing together the forgotten history of a fallen regional warrior. It functions as a narrative-heavy excursion that rewards lore enthusiasts with an incredibly fast-striking weapon.

### The Vrakhir
- **Location:** Unknown (Tied to Lacra's Progression)
- **Requirement:** Complete "The Night of Horrors" (Optional Quest).

Tied directly to Lacra, one of the game's more mysterious companions, this sword is obtained by progressing her optional storyline, "The Night of Horrors." Given the explicit naming convention of the blade, it has strong ties to the ancient vampire origins Rebel Wolves established in the backstory. If you are actively pursuing Lacra's storyline, this is an unmissable and highly powerful reward. For more on maneuvering her specific dialogue options, consult our [Builds and Skill Trees](/guides/builds-and-skill-trees/) strategies to ensure your stats pass the necessary checks.

## Exploration and Time-Sensitive Discoveries

Rebel Wolves made sure the time of day drastically alters the sandbox. Some weapons can only be found if you are willing to face the severe threats that emerge after the sun sets.

### Gladius
- **Location:** Parlita (Mount Balaor)
- **Requirement:** Found in a cave chest; must be visited at night.

The **Gladius** is hidden away in a remote, freezing cave on Mount Balaor, bordering the Parlita region. However, community explorers report that you can only safely navigate the interior mechanics of this specific cave system during the night. Nighttime exploration inherently means dealing with heightened enemy aggression, forcing players to rely heavily on shifting into their vampire form to survive the encounter. 

### The Kingslayer
- **Location:** Svartrau City
- **Requirement:** Found in a chest along the riverbank.

Unlike the Gladius, **The Kingslayer** is a pure exploration reward that lacks a strict time-of-day requirement. It is reportedly hidden inside an unassuming chest tucked away along the muddy riverbank just outside the towering walls of Svartrau City. It pays off significantly to scour the outer perimeters of major settlements before rushing straight through the main gates.

## Merchant and Skill-Gated Swords

Not all legendary armaments are guarded by ancient beasts or buried deep in tombs; some simply require deep pockets, while others demand highly specific character stats to even appear in a vendor's inventory.

### The Runner Up
- **Location:** Svartrau
- **Requirement:** Purchased from the blacksmith near the Cathedral.

This is a straightforward acquisition assuming you have enough currency. The local blacksmith stationed near the Svartrau Cathedral sells this reliable blade. It serves as an excellent mid-game upgrade for players who prefer to spend their coin rather than grind through optional dungeons.

### The Contender
- **Location:** Tantari Village
- **Requirement:** Purchased from the Tantari blacksmith.

Operating similarly to The Runner Up, **The Contender** is readily available for purchase from the main blacksmith operating in Tantari Village. Tantari acts as a crucial hub for upgrading standard physical gear, making this weapon a solid baseline choice for players focusing purely on standard stamina-based swordplay.

### The Prizewinner
- **Location:** Briar Sloughs (Uraishi Camp)
- **Requirement:** Purchased from the Uraishi Blacksmith (Requires *Bewitching Influence* perk).

This specific weapon highlights where the game’s underlying RPG mechanics truly shine. **The Prizewinner** cannot be bought by just anyone with a full coin purse. It is sold exclusively by the Uraishi Blacksmith situated in the Briar Sloughs, but he will reportedly only offer his premium stock if Coen has unlocked the *Bewitching Influence* Witchcraft perk. This limitation mandates that you invest points directly into the Witchcraft skill tree to magically manipulate or charm the vendor. It is a brilliant design choice that heavily rewards players leaning into magical, charismatic builds over raw strength.

## Optimizing Your Arsenal

Locating a Legendary Sword is only the baseline requirement. To maximize their damage output on higher difficulty settings, you must pair them with appropriate hex modifiers and synergistic armor sets. Fast-striking blades like The Kingslayer pair exceptionally well with quick-cast hexes that interrupt enemy animations, while heavy hitters like Durandal require crowd-control magic that pins targets firmly in place. 

As the player base continues to dissect the sprawling map, more hidden caches and secret gear are expected to surface. Manage your Infamy carefully, keep a close eye on the day-night cycle, and never underestimate the value of conversational perks when dealing with local merchants.
