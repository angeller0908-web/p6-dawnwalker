Rebel Wolves and publisher Bandai Namco turned heads at Summer Game Fest 2026 with a bold promotional strategy for their upcoming dark fantasy action RPG, *The Blood of Dawnwalker*. Instead of just showing off combat or world details, the new trailer laid out plans for a multi-era franchise. It teased a future sequel that reportedly moves the vampire narrative from the historical Carpathians to the 21st century.

While some fans are thrilled by this narrative confidence, the trailer has also sparked debate in community spaces like Reddit. Some players complain that the trailer's intro potentially spoils the ending of the first game, which is still months away from its [September 3 release date](/guides/launch-date-september-3-confirmed/).

## The Spoiler Controversy
In community forums, players are debating whether the new trailer gives away too much. The intro shows scenes hinting at how the initial chapter in Vale Sangora concludes, prompting warnings from fans who suggest avoiding the trailer to preserve the first play-through.

It is unusual for a studio to hint at the outcome of an unreleased game. However, Rebel Wolves appears to be banking on the journey rather than the destination. For a choice-driven game, the exact state of the world at the end of the game might vary, meaning the trailer's \"spoiler\" could represent only one potential branch of the story.

## A Bold Sequel Tease
Revealing plans for a sequel before the first game launches is a high-stakes move. Some community members view it as a sign of absolute confidence in the project's quality and its appeal to fans of dark fantasy RPGs, particularly those looking for a spiritual successor to [The Witcher 3](/guides/vs-the-witcher-3/). The planned shift from the historical setting of the Carpathians to a modern-day setting—previously hinted at in a recent [CGI teaser](/guides/2026-06-06-dawnwalker-cgi-teaser-hints-at-modern-day-era-for-rebel-wolves-vampire/)—suggests a massive shift in scope for the Dawnwalker universe.

## Pricing and Competition in a Crowded Fall Window
The marketing push comes as the industry gears up for a highly competitive autumn window. Community discussions highlight *The Blood of Dawnwalker*'s standard $69.99 pricing. With several high-profile action titles and RPGs launching within the same month, players are already planning their budgets. Some online retailers are already offering slight pre-order discounts to attract early buyers.

For players waiting to see how the combat and world hold up, the developer's willingness to tease a grander saga shows they are playing the long game.
