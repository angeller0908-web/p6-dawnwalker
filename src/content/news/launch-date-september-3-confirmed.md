Rebel Wolves and publisher Bandai Namco have confirmed that **The Blood of Dawnwalker launches on September 3, 2026**, simultaneously on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam. Pre-orders are live across every platform.

## Why the date matters

A early-September slot is a confident pick. It lands ahead of the crowded October–November holiday rush, giving Rebel Wolves' debut room to breathe and reviewers time to dig into a long RPG before bigger sequels arrive. For a brand-new IP without an established fanbase, that breathing room is valuable — it's the same window several sleeper RPG hits have used to build momentum through word of mouth.

## What's included at launch

The game ships in Standard, Eclipse, Day One, and Collector's editions, with bonuses ranging from a digital soundtrack and comic up to a 9-inch Coen figurine in the top-tier box. We compare them all in our [editions and pre-order guide](/guides/release-date-platforms-editions/).
