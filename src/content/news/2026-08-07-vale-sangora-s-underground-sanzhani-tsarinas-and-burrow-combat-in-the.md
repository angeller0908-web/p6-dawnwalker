Vale Sangora is shaping up to be one of the most hostile and intricately designed settings in recent RPG history. As we approach the September 3, 2026 release of *The Blood of Dawnwalker*, the development team at Rebel Wolves continues to reveal the horrors that inhabit their paranormal low-fantasy world. While much of the early marketing has focused on the sweeping gothic cityscapes, faction politics, and the internal struggle of the protagonist's dual nature, recent lore snippets from the community have turned our attention downward—straight into the dirt.

The underground ecosystems of Vale Sangora are reportedly teeming with life, and not all of it is friendly. Among the moles, mice, and rabbits that dig through the soil, a unique and dangerous race known as the Sanzhani has established a subterranean empire. Based on the latest community lore drops, these creatures present a fascinating twist on traditional folklore, completely ignoring human currency in favor of something far more macabre: human teeth.

This editorial examines the known lore surrounding the Sanzhani, their mysterious rulers known as Tsarinas, and how players might realistically encounter these burrow-dwelling threats during their journey across Vale Sangora.

## The Sanzhani: A Grounded Take on Dark Folklore

Rebel Wolves is heavily staffed by industry veterans who previously shaped the continent of *The Witcher 3*, and their penchant for grounding fantasy creatures in harsh, believable ecosystems is clearly visible in the design of the Sanzhani. According to recent lore passages shared by the community, the Sanzhani are described simply and ominously: "They are small, but so are hornets."

This comparison to hornets is crucial for understanding how they will function mechanically. It tells us that the danger of the Sanzhani likely does not come from their individual physical strength, but from their numbers and their relentless aggression. In many high-fantasy games, underground dwellers are depicted as robust dwarven societies or solitary, massive monsters. *The Blood of Dawnwalker* takes a distinctly different route, presenting a race that shares its burrows with common woodland critters. This design choice anchors the Sanzhani in the natural world of Vale Sangora, making them feel like a parasitic extension of the environment rather than a wholly alien or overly fantastical threat.

Their defining characteristic is their complete disinterest in what humans typically value. Gold, silver, and mastercrafted weapons mean nothing to them. Instead, they hoard human teeth. While previous coverage briefly touched upon the rumors of [macabre tooth fairies](/guides/2026-07-25-the-blood-of-dawnwalker-macabre-tooth-fairies-time-bending-magic-and-a/), the confirmation of the Sanzhani provides a concrete name and a defined habitat for these entities. Their obsession with teeth strips away the whimsical western notion of a benevolent fairy leaving coins under a pillow, positioning them instead as violent scavengers who might not patiently wait for a tooth to naturally fall out before claiming it.

## The Tsarinas and Subterranean Society

The stolen teeth are not kept for personal adornment by the common Sanzhani foot soldiers. The latest lore explicitly confirms that these items are hoarded and offered as gifts to their rulers, who are titled "Tsarinas."

The use of the title "Tsarina" carries heavy Slavic connotations, reinforcing the Eastern European mythological roots that the developers at Rebel Wolves are known for drawing upon. It also heavily implies a matriarchal hierarchy, possibly resembling a hive structure given the earlier comparison to hornets. If the Sanzhani operate like a colony of aggressive insects, the Tsarina would be the equivalent of a queen, safely entrenched deep within the earth, directing her subjects to harvest specific resources from the human population above ground.

For players charting their path through the [Vale Sangora world](/guides/vale-sangora-world-guide/), this societal structure strongly hints at how quest lines involving the Sanzhani might unfold. It is highly expected that players will not just fight random mobs of these creatures in the open woods. Instead, you will likely need to locate the hidden entrances to their intricate burrow networks, navigate the cramped, lightless underground, and eventually face off against a Tsarina in her central chamber. Whether these encounters are mandatory story beats or part of the rumored subterranean investigation side activities remains unconfirmed, but the world-building lays a clear structural foundation for multi-stage dungeon crawling.

## Expected Combat Dynamics in the Burrows

Fighting creatures that operate as a literal swarm presents unique challenges for the game's combat system. We know that *The Blood of Dawnwalker* features a distinct rhythm to its swordplay, heavily reliant on precise spacing, parries, and timing. However, fighting a swarm of small, aggressive enemies in a confined space will likely render traditional dueling tactics ineffective.

When dealing with the Sanzhani, players investing in specific [builds and skill trees](/guides/builds-and-skill-trees/) are expected to rely heavily on crowd control and area-of-effect abilities. This is where [combat and hex magic](/guides/combat-and-hex-magic/) will likely take precedence over physical strikes. Using hexes that ignite the ground, emit concussive blasts, or create toxic barriers would be far more effective against a swarm than attempting to slash individual attackers in the dark.

Furthermore, the claustrophobic nature of the burrows might heavily influence your tactical choices regarding your form. *The Blood of Dawnwalker* allows players to transition between a human form during the day and a powerful, predatory vampire form at night. The heightened senses of the vampire form could provide a significant advantage in the pitch-black tunnels, allowing you to track the Sanzhani before they strike. 

However, given the game's emphasis on the passage of time, one must wonder if the Sanzhani behave differently during the day versus the night. Do they retreat deeper into the earth when the sun is up, making daytime exploration of their burrows safer? Or does the absence of sunlight in the underground mean their aggression is constant regardless of the hour? These environmental mechanics are expected to play a major role in how players plan their expeditions into the dirt.

## The Economy of Teeth

Why do the Tsarinas want teeth in the first place? In low-fantasy settings, items like bones, blood, and teeth often hold significant magical resonance or ritualistic value. It is entirely possible that the Tsarinas use human teeth as a focal point for their own crude earth magic, or perhaps they construct their subterranean thrones from the enamel of Vale Sangora's unfortunate citizens.

From a gameplay perspective, this raises interesting questions about loot and economy. Will players be able to harvest teeth from fallen human enemies to use as a bartering tool or a distraction when entering Sanzhani territory? Giving players the ability to trade with a Tsarina, offering her high-quality teeth in exchange for rare underground resources, would perfectly align with the game's promise of a reactive, narrative sandbox. While Rebel Wolves has not officially confirmed a functional "tooth economy," the deep focus on varied, choice-driven interactions suggests that violence might not be the only way to navigate Sanzhani encounters.

## Community Watch: Pre-Order Precautions

Switching gears from in-game lore to real-world logistics, we have an important advisory for our readers looking to secure their copy ahead of the fast-approaching September release.

A recent report from the community has highlighted severe customer service issues with the international retailer e2z. A player who attempted to cancel and subsequently reverse the cancellation of their pre-order reported a complete lack of communication for over a month, with no refund processed and no game secured. While tempting 10% discounts are always appealing—especially given the ongoing industry debates surrounding [AA vs AAA pricing](/guides/2026-06-29-the-blood-of-dawnwalker-s-70-pricing-sparks-aa-vs-aaa-debate/)—we strongly advise sticking to established, highly reputable retailers for your pre-orders. 

Stores like Amazon, your local verified game shops, or direct digital purchases via the Steam, PlayStation, and Xbox storefronts remain the absolute safest methods to ensure you gain access on day one without risking your funds. As always, exercise extreme caution when dealing with lesser-known storefronts offering deals that seem too good to be true, particularly when customer support channels appear abandoned.

## Preparing for the Dark

The revelation of the Sanzhani and their Tsarinas adds another welcome layer of dread to the already oppressive atmosphere of Vale Sangora. It serves as a stark reminder that threats in *The Blood of Dawnwalker* do not just come from the grand political maneuvering of vampire lords or the religious zealotry of the surface factions. Sometimes, the most immediate danger is waiting just beneath the soil, listening for the footsteps of anyone foolish enough to wander the woods unprepared.

We will continue to monitor the community spaces and official channels for more information on the Sanzhani and the other terrifying entities that populate this grim world. As we edge closer to launch day, mastering the nuances of these underground ecosystems will be critical for anyone hoping to survive the coming darkness.
