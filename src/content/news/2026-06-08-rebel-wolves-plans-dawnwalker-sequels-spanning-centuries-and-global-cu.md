Rebel Wolves is looking far beyond the boundaries of Vale Sangora. While players are preparing for the September 2026 release of *The Blood of Dawnwalker*, reports indicate the Polish studio already has future installments mapped out. These planned sequels will reportedly move away from the initial dark fantasy setting, exploring different centuries and distinct global cultures.

This revelation sheds new light on the recent [modern-day CGI teaser](/guides/2026-06-06-dawnwalker-cgi-teaser-hints-at-modern-day-era-for-rebel-wolves-vampire/) shown during the Summer Game Fest. Rather than representing the immediate setting of the first game, the teaser serves as a broader statement of intent for where the franchise is headed.

## A Multigenerational Vampire Saga

According to reports, Rebel Wolves intends to build a franchise that spans multiple historical eras. Each sequel is expected to draw heavily on the local myths, folklore, and vampire traditions of its respective setting. This approach would allow the developers to shift from the classic gothic themes of *The Blood of Dawnwalker* to entirely different cultural backdrops.

For fans debating the unexpected modern elements in the recent [SGF trailer](/guides/2026-06-07-dawnwalker-sgf-trailer-sparks-spoiler-debates-and-modern-era-speculati/), this structural plan offers some clarity. It suggests that while the debut game remains focused on the dark fantasy world of Vale Sangora, the IP itself is designed to evolve across time. Some community members suggest the modern-day teaser was primarily designed as a pitch for investors and publishers to demonstrate the long-term viability of the IP, rather than representing active gameplay footage from the upcoming September release.

## Pre-Order Details: Standard vs. Eclipse Editions

Alongside the franchise roadmap, Rebel Wolves confirmed details regarding the game's digital editions. Players looking to secure their copy ahead of the September launch can choose between two versions:

*   **Standard Edition:** Includes the base game.
*   **Eclipse Edition:** Includes the base game, the digital soundtrack, a world compendium, and a digital comic book.

For players interested in the deep lore of this new dark fantasy universe, the Eclipse Edition's world compendium and comic book will likely provide essential background reading before exploring the game's mechanics. You can find more details on how these editions compare in our [release editions breakdown](/guides/release-date-platforms-editions/).

With the release date set for September 3, 2026, Rebel Wolves is positioning *The Blood of Dawnwalker* not just as a standalone debut, but as the foundation for a much larger, time-spanning franchise. We will continue to track official updates as the developer shares more about its launch plans.
