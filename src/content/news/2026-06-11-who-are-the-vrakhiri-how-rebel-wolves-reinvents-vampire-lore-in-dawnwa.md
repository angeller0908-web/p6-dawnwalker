When Rebel Wolves first announced *The Blood of Dawnwalker*, dark fantasy enthusiasts immediately drew comparisons to legendary gothic RPGs. However, recent details regarding the game's primary antagonists, the **Vrakhiri**, indicate that the developers are not just treading familiar ground. They are reconstructing vampire biology and mythology from scratch. 

In the alternate 14th-century Carpathian basin, these creatures are far from the traditional nocturnal counts hiding in isolated castles. Instead, they have stepped out of the shadows to establish a tyrannical dominion over the valley of [Vale Sangora](/guides/vale-sangora-world-guide/), treating the surviving human population as a organized herd.

## Subverting the Vampire Mythos

Traditional vampire tropes—such as needing an invitation to cross a doorway, being repelled by garlic, or being unable to cross running water—reportedly do not apply to the Vrakhiri. The developers at Rebel Wolves, comprised of seasoned veterans behind the Witcher series, are crafting a more predatory, biological breed of monster. 

Some of the key physical and lore differences expected for the Vrakhiri include:
- **Anatomy**: Instead of simple retractable fangs, their jaws are described as featuring needle-like, serrated teeth resembling an anglerfish, which grow more monstrous and numerous as they age.
- **The Turning Ritual**: The Vrakhiri do not turn their victims with a simple bite to the neck. Instead, the transformation is triggered by staking one of their own mineralized teeth directly through the victim's heart.
- **Vulnerabilities**: While traditional wards are useless, silver remains a lethal threat to their flesh, and direct sunlight will scorch them, forcing their operations to remain strictly nocturnal.

## The Patriarch and His Thralls

The Vrakhiri hierarchy is commanded by [Brencis](/guides/brencis-and-vampire-officers/), a Roman-era ancient vampire whose origins date back to the second century. He coordinates the clan's expansion, utilizing both physical brutality and political manipulation. 

To maintain their grip on Vale Sangora, the Vrakhiri rely on the Blood Guards. These are human thralls who have willingly undergone dark rituals, binding their minds to their masters to enforce the Vrakhiri's laws. It is under this shadow of servitude that the protagonist, Coen, finds himself caught. As a silver miner turned half-vampire hybrid, Coen must use both human swordplay and stolen vampiric attributes to resist the clan's influence and save his family.

## Day and Night Duality

This biological split is reflected directly in the gameplay mechanics. During the daytime, players must focus on tactical human swordplay, directional blocking, and defensive [hex magic](/guides/combat-and-hex-magic/). 

When night falls, Coen's vampiric blood takes over, allowing him to use supernatural traversal and combat tools. These night-specific powers, detailed in our [vampire powers breakdown](/guides/vampire-powers-and-wolf-form/), include Shadowstep teleportation and direct claw strikes to regenerate health, mirroring the raw aggression of his Vrakhiri oppressors. 

## High Demand for the Collector's Edition

As anticipation builds for the September 3, 2026 launch, the excitement has spilled over into retail. Community reports on [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/DawnwalkerOfficial/comments/1u1wu33/i_almost_got_my_head_torn_off_today_because_my/) suggest that pre-orders for the limited physical Collector's Edition are selling rapidly. 

The physical edition is highly sought after because it features a 23cm PureArts figurine of Coen, alongside a 60-page hardcover World Compendium detailing the dark history of the Vrakhiri. With limited stock expected at launch, fans looking to secure these physical collectibles are advised to monitor retail listings closely as the autumn release window approaches.
