As anticipation builds for the September 3, 2026 release of Rebel Wolves' dark fantasy action-RPG, the community is already identifying standout figures in the game's vampire-dominated hierarchy. Among the lieutenants serving the vampire overlord Brencis, the youngest officer, Ambrus, has captured significant attention. A recent community spotlight has highlighted his ruthless role in Vale Sangora and the viral phrase associated with his dark personality: "Smile, you're ruining the atmosphere."

## Who is Ambrus?
Ambrus serves as one of the three principal lieutenants under Brencis, alongside Xanthe and Bakir, as detailed in our overview of [Brencis and his vampire officers](/guides/brencis-and-vampire-officers/). Unlike older vampires who carry centuries of nobility or combat experience, Ambrus is the youngest member of the pack. 

According to developer lore, Ambrus endured a miserable and painful human existence before receiving the vampire kiss. This traumatic background left him with a desperate, lingering need to prove himself to his peers. Once turned, he embraced his new power with absolute cruelty. He is described as highly ambitious, vain, and entirely willing to cross any moral boundary to secure his standing. In the gothic territory of [Vale Sangora](/guides/vale-sangora-world-guide/), Ambrus reportedly controls the human trafficking trade, managing the supply of blood that feeds the vampire elite.

## The Story Behind the Meme
The phrase "Smile, you're ruining the atmosphere" has quickly turned into a popular meme across platforms like Reddit and YouTube. Community discussions suggest this quote represents the chilling contrast of Ambrus’s character. He is often depicted wearing ornate, polished armor with perfectly styled hair—an image of clean, noble authority—yet he participates in the most horrific aspects of the vampire regime.

Fans speculate that this phrase highlights his casual cruelty, dismissing the horror of his blood trade with a smirk. The contrast between his polished noble appearance and his dark actions has made him a fast favorite for players interested in the game's narrative sandbox.

## Dealing with Ambrus in Vale Sangora
Because *The Blood of Dawnwalker* features a choice-driven narrative sandbox, players taking on the role of Coen are expected to have multiple ways to handle Ambrus. With the game's focus on choice and consequence, you might choose to actively disrupt his human trafficking network, align with his ambitions to gain political power, or attempt to play him off against the other lieutenants.

There is also intense speculation on the [romance guide](/guides/romance-guide/) forums regarding whether Ambrus will be a romanceable option, given his complex backstory and high community popularity. While Rebel Wolves has not confirmed which vampire officers support romance paths, the studio's focus on deep character interactions makes him a prime candidate for complex relationship mechanics. 

Whether you seek to end his reign of terror or exploit his ambition, Ambrus is shaping up to be one of the most compelling antagonists in Coen's journey.
