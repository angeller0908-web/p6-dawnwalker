When Rebel Wolves first dropped their promotional teasers, RPG enthusiasts were left questioning how a gritty medieval world could coexist with modern technology. Now, the Polish studio has cleared the air, confirming how these eras tie together and revealing a save-carrying feature designed for a multi-game saga.

## A Multi-Era RPG Saga with Persistent Saves
Rebel Wolves has officially confirmed that *The Blood of Dawnwalker* is merely the starting point of an ambitious multi-game saga. In a recent interview, the developers revealed that they are building a franchise designed to span different historical eras, and crucially, players will be able to carry their save files and narrative decisions over into subsequent titles. 

This approach directly addresses how the studio plans to handle their previously teased [sequel plans](/guides/2026-06-08-rebel-wolves-plans-dawnwalker-sequels-spanning-centuries-and-global-cu/). Instead of resetting the state of the world with each installment, your choices in Vale Sangora will reportedly shape the sociopolitical landscape of the vampire-dominated world as it progresses through the centuries. While the exact technical details of how these save transfers will function across potential console generations remain unconfirmed, the promise of a continuous, choice-driven narrative stretching across different time periods is a massive hook for fans of deep role-playing games.

## The Modern-Day CGI Teaser is Actually the Post-Credits Scene
When the studio released [the CGI teaser](/guides/2026-06-06-dawnwalker-cgi-teaser-hints-at-modern-day-era-for-rebel-wolves-vampire/), it ignited heavy [modern-era speculation](/guides/2026-06-07-dawnwalker-sgf-trailer-sparks-spoiler-debates-and-modern-era-speculati/) and split the community. Many players worried that the game’s core dark fantasy aesthetic would be suddenly interrupted by modern technology.

However, Rebel Wolves has cleared the air with a surprising revelation: the controversial modern-day footage is actually the game’s post-credits scene. 

By confirming that the modern-day elements are reserved for the post-credits sequence, the developers have implicitly reassured fans that the main campaign of *The Blood of Dawnwalker* will remain grounded in its low-fantasy, gothic medieval setting. The modern-day twist serves as a narrative bridge, setting the stage for future sequels that will leap forward in time while carrying over the consequences of your medieval exploits.

## What This Means for Vale Sangora and Beyond
This structured transition explains how Rebel Wolves plans to balance their gritty medieval lore with a broader, centuries-spanning story. Players can focus on mastering [combat and hex magic](/guides/combat-and-hex-magic/) and navigating the politics of Vale Sangora without worrying about sudden, immersion-breaking shifts to modern times mid-game. 

Instead, the modern era acts as the ultimate payoff—a glimpse into the far-reaching consequences of your actions that will directly feed into the next chapter of the saga. With the release date set for September 3, 2026, the studio is setting up a narrative framework that feels uniquely ambitious, treating their debut game not just as a standalone experience, but as the foundation of a generational vampire epic.
