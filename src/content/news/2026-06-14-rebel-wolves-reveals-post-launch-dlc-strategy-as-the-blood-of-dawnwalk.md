The anticipation for Rebel Wolves' debut RPG continues to build ahead of its autumn release. According to community reports and recent developer updates, *The Blood of Dawnwalker* has officially surpassed 1.5 million wishlists across target platforms. In tandem with this milestone, the studio has outlined its post-launch content strategy, promising a series of smaller, free DLCs to support players after the September launch.

This strategy mirrors the post-launch support pattern seen in other major dark fantasy RPGs, drawing comparisons to how the developers handled extra content in [The Witcher 3](/guides/vs-the-witcher-3/). 

## Free DLCs Planned for Post-Launch Support

According to reports, Rebel Wolves intends to release several smaller add-ons at no extra cost to players. While the exact nature of these DLCs has not been confirmed, they are expected to include minor expansions to the game's equipment pool, alternative character cosmetics, or self-contained side quests. 

This decision aligns with the studio’s player-first philosophy. Rather than locking small cosmetic upgrades behind paywalls, the team aims to keep the community engaged as they explore the gothic valleys of Vale Sangora. It remains unclear whether larger, paid expansions are also in the pipeline, but the initial post-launch phase will focus on these free drops. 

For a breakdown of what to expect at launch, check out the [everything we know guide](/guides/everything-we-know/) and details on [release platforms and editions](/guides/release-date-platforms-editions/).

## Hitting the 1.5 Million Wishlist Milestone

The announcement of the free DLC plans coincides with the game surpassing 1.5 million wishlists. This milestone highlights the massive interest in Rebel Wolves' debut project, especially given the pedigree of the development team, which features veterans of CD Projekt Red.

Much of the community excitement revolves around the game’s core mechanics. Players on Reddit have described the upcoming title as a "dark fantasy Blade RPG," referencing the expected blend of swordplay and supernatural traits. The dual nature of the protagonist—balancing human vulnerabilities during the day and vampire strength at night—promises a unique gameplay loop. You can learn more about these mechanics in our overview of [vampire powers and forms](/guides/vampire-powers-and-wolf-form/) and the [combat and hex magic systems](/guides/combat-and-hex-magic/).

## A Saga Destined to Outgrow the Middle Ages

While the initial game is set in a medieval dark fantasy setting, the studio has broader ambitions. It was recently confirmed that *The Blood of Dawnwalker* is intended to launch a multi-game saga that will eventually move past the Middle Ages. The overarching story is expected to span centuries, potentially leading to modern-era settings in future installments. 

This long-term vision makes the initial entry critical in establishing the lore of the Vrakhiri and the rules of this supernatural world. For more on the narrative foundation, read about the [sequel plans spanning centuries](/guides/2026-06-08-rebel-wolves-plans-dawnwalker-sequels-spanning-centuries-and-global-cu).

*The Blood of Dawnwalker* is set to release on September 3, 2026.
