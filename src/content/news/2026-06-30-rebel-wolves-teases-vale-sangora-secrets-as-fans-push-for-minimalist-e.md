Rebel Wolves has shared a new teaser from its ongoing promotion, offering another glimpse into the dark setting of its debut dark fantasy RPG. Titled "Postcards from Vale Sangora," the teaser highlights the dangers awaiting players inside the game's ancient ruins, prompting the community to voice their expectations for how exploration should be handled.

The teaser description warns players that while the ancient gates of Vale Sangora hide treasures and long-lost mysteries, players will not be the only ones wandering these forgotten halls.

## Who Else Walks the Forgotten Halls?

The warning about other wanderers has fueled speculations regarding the encounter design. In a world populated by rival factions, dangerous beasts, and the ancient [Vrakhiri](/guides/who-are-the-vrakhiri-how-rebel-wolves-reinvents-vampire-lore-in-dawnwa/), players expectedly need to stay alert even during quiet exploration segments. 

It is community-reported that these ruins might feature dynamic encounter zones, where hostile entities—ranging from rogue vampires to specialized monster hunters—patrol the corridors. Rather than serving as static dungeons, the subterranean environments of [Vale Sangora](/guides/vale-sangora-world-guide/) appear to function as active, hostile ecosystems.

## The Markerless Exploration Debate

Following the teaser, fans on community hubs discussed how they want to navigate these gothic environments. A popular suggestion among players is the removal of traditional quest markers and map indicators, drawing comparisons to how recent open-world titles like *Elden Ring* handle discovery.

Many hope Rebel Wolves avoids filling the screen with icons and question marks, a design choice often associated with older open-world RPGs. Instead, fans argue that relying on physical landmarks, journal entries, and environmental storytelling fits the tone of a dark fantasy vampire sandbox. 

Given the team's pedigree in crafting choices that shape a [narrative sandbox](/guides/2026-06-17-what-a-narrative-sandbox-means-for-the-blood-of-dawnwalker/), a navigation system that respects player curiosity could elevate the sense of mystery. However, Rebel Wolves has not yet confirmed the final configuration of the game's HUD or map mechanics.
