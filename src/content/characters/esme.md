**Esme** is **[Coen](/characters/coen/)**'s mother, and part of the reason he never left Vale Sangora to chase the adventures he dreamed about as a boy. His family needed him, so he stayed and worked the silver mines beside his father.

## The family at the heart of the story

Coen is the **eldest of four children**, and his refusal to abandon his family is the emotional core of the whole game. When his sister **[Lunka](/characters/lunka/)** is dying and the vampire lord **[Brencis](/characters/brencis/)** turns his "mercy" into a hostage situation, it's the survival of people like Esme that gives Coen's roughly **30-day** quest its urgency.

## Why she matters to the player

Characters like Esme are why Dawnwalker frames its power fantasy as a tragedy. Coen becomes a vampire by night not for glory but to protect a household — and every time you embrace the monster for more power, you're trading away a piece of the humanity that ties him to home.

For the full picture of the family and the valley they live in, see our [Story, Setting & Characters guide](/guides/story-setting-characters/) and the [Vale Sangora world guide](/guides/vale-sangora-world-guide/).
